# 2026-11-15 Tube clip

New part, independent of the RM10 hanger of
session/2026-11-15-rm10-shelf-hanger.md.

## Done

- `scripts/tube_clip.scad`: parametric snap clip for a 20 mm tube,
  taped under a shelf. 60 x 40 x 3 mm pad, C ring with 2.4 mm wall
  and a 17 mm mouth to the side, solid nose above the mouth.
- Screw holes added as a second fixing: one 3.8 mm hole per side of
  the ring, 22.5 mm from the tube axis, countersunk 7.5 mm from
  below. `screws=false` leaves them out.
- `make tube-clip` builds `out/tube-clip.stl` (two clips) and four
  previews; `make tube-clip-save` copies the STL to `models/`.
- `models/tube-clip.stl` saved: 60 x 63.2 x 40 mm as exported,
  24.7 cm3 for both clips, watertight, 0.3 cm2 of overhang (the
  horizontal screw holes).
- `scripts/tube_edge_clip.scad`: second model, pushed onto the front
  edge of the 8 mm shelf. Web, 48 mm plate, 3 mm jaw with a pinch
  bump, the same ring under the plate, mouth to the front.
  `make tube-edge-clip`, `make tube-edge-clip-save`;
  `models/tube-edge-clip.stl` saved: two clips, 52 x 85.8 x 30 mm as
  exported, 30.5 cm3, watertight, no overhang.
- specs/tube-clip.md written; printability results added.
- `make check` passes.

## Decisions

- Mouth to the side: the load rests on the closed half of the ring,
  not on the snap.
- The mouth is cut before the corners are filleted and only below the
  pad. Cutting it afterwards left a sliver between the upper lip and
  the pad.
- Wall 2.4 mm instead of 3: about 45 N on the lip instead of 85 N,
  strain about 1 %. Only the lower arm bends.
- Pad 60 x 40 mm for 24 cm2 of tape per clip, about 0.85 kg for the
  pair by the 55 cm2 per kg rule for acrylic foam tape.
- Printed standing on an end face: no supports, layers run around the
  ring.
- Two clips in one STL (`count`).
- Screw holes at mid length, one per side: the driver clears ring
  and tube by 10 mm, so screws can be added later with the tube in
  place. Countersunk for a flush head; 1.15 mm of pad stays under it,
  enough for a backup to the tape. Screw length is limited by the
  shelf: L - 3 mm enters it.
- Edge clip in line with the taped clip: tube 5 mm + `tape_t`
  (1.0 mm) under the shelf, axis 32 mm behind the edge so the 60 mm
  pad of a taped clip on the same axis stays behind the edge.
- Edge clip: slot corners stay square (web and jaw are added after
  the fillets) so a square shelf edge seats fully. Grip comes from a
  bump that bends the jaw 0.8 mm, about 7 N, not from a tight slot.
- Edge clip mouth to the front: inserting the tube pushes the clip
  on.
